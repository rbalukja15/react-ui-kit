DataTable: rows can open a record on click (`onRowClick`), skipping clicks on the row's actions, checkbox and links, and a click that ends a text selection. Columns can sort descending first (`sortFirst`), take a responsive `width` and style their header and cells with `cellSx`. New `actionsWidth`, `tableSx` and `containerSx` props. A new page now scrolls the rows back to the top, and a pinned header keeps a focused row from scrolling under it.
